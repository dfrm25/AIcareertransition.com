AI use cases at work: example proof receipts

An AI use case you can prove at work is one weekly task, done with an approved tool, timed before and after, and checked by a human before anything is shared.

Every number below is an Example, not a real result. Your receipt uses your own log.

Your path: 101 → 201 → proof

Marketing: weekly campaign recap

Task: Weekly performance recap for the team channel.

Tool: Microsoft 365 Copilot in Excel and Chat (Work IQ on for last week's thread).

Before: Example: 90 minutes pulling numbers and writing by hand.

After: Example: 35 minutes including review.

Human check: Totals matched to the source export; claims about causes marked as hypotheses.

Outcome: Example: recap ships Monday by 10am; resume line drafted from 8 logged runs.

Analytics: monthly variance note

Task: Explain plan vs actual for the monthly review.

Tool: Copilot in Excel plus prompt P2 (Variance explanation).

Before: Example: 3 hours across two workbooks.

After: Example: 75 minutes including a hand spot-check.

Human check: Drivers sum to total variance; two totals recalculated by hand.

Outcome: Example: zero numbers corrected after sending across 3 months.

Product: feedback synthesis

Task: Turn a month of customer feedback into themes for planning.

Tool: Copilot with the feedback file attached ("Use this file only") and a one-job agent.

Before: Example: a full day tagging feedback by hand.

After: Example: 2 hours including review of every theme.

Human check: Five quotes per theme traced back to the source; no invented counts.

Outcome: Example: themes used in the planning review; agent reused monthly.

Operations: weekly ops status and checklist

Task: Weekly ops status plus a checklist for a recurring handoff.

Tool: Copilot Chat (prompts P1 and P10) with Work IQ on for meeting notes.

Before: Example: 60 minutes for status; handoff steps lived in one person's head.

After: Example: 25 minutes for status; a written checklist with approval points.

Human check: Owners confirmed their items; checklist walked through once with the team.

Outcome: Example: handoff errors tracked weekly; checklist becomes the team's standard.

These are illustrations, not client results. No testimonials. Build your own with the Weekly proof log on artifacts.html.
