Methodology

  • Opt-in web sample, not a probability panel. We will say that in paragraph one of the report.
  • Weight by role, seniority, and region. Publish effective n and design effect.
  • No causal claims on pay. T1 is a relative band, not matched payroll.
  • Competency is scored from behaviors (schema, eval, agents, governance), not from “I am advanced.” Same L1–L5 as the PM guide.
  • Release: HTML report + CSV of aggregate tables + embeddable charts with required source line. Microdata stays off-site.
  • Screener: employed in knowledge work, 20+ hours/week.

How the sample is built

Primary: this form, plus the homepage quiz (“help build the benchmark”). Secondary: partner newsletters with a unique UTM and a function-cut they can republish. Incentive is the early PDF, not a raffle. Speed and duplicate checks on.
