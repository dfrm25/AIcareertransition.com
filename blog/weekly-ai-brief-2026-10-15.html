This Week in AI, Oct 15, 2026: Argon is global

Reviewed Oct 15, 2026: Gemini 4 Argon is globally available for developers, enterprises, and Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers.

Official vendor sources only. This is a career brief, not a news dump and not a university catalog.

Gemini 4 Argon is globally available

On 13 October 2026 Google made Gemini 4 Argon globally available for developers, enterprises, and Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers. Muse Wonder and the Gemini agent stay on the brief.

OpenAI

GPT-6.1 Sol is an upgrade to GPT-6 Sol

OpenAI introduced GPT-6.1 Sol as an upgrade to GPT-6 Sol. OpenAI says it nearly matches GPT-6 Astra on agentic coding, computer use, and professional work. It is available to Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, and Edu users in ChatGPT Work and Codex, and it is not yet available in Chat. Developers get it in the API as gpt-6.1-sol.

Google

Oct 13, 2026

Gemini 4 Argon is globally available

On 13 October 2026 Google made Gemini 4 Argon globally available. Developers and enterprises get it through the API in AI Studio and the Gemini platform, and enterprises can use Gemini for business. Google AI Ultra subscribers get it in Google Antigravity. Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers get it in the Gemini app and AI Mode. It is for complex work across software engineering, enterprise knowledge work such as legal and finance, cybersecurity, and science. The Gemini app adds a toggle from chat to tasks.

Meta

Oct 12, 2026

Muse Wonder is Meta's most capable pair

On 12 October 2026 Meta introduced Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, its most capable models, for personal and professional agent tasks. Active Memory carries context across conversations. Active Focus keeps working toward a goal and checks in before important actions. Muse Wonder is available now through the Meta Model API and Muse Code. Muse Wonder Full is for Muse Code subscribers. Muse Wonder will later power Muse, rolling out first to subscribers.

Try this:

Before trusting any agent's memory at work, write down three facts it should carry over, then check in a second session whether it recalled them correctly. You stay the check, and follow your company's AI policy. Go

Google

Oct 8, 2026

Gemini agent is a universal agent for work

On 8 October 2026, Google Cloud announced the Gemini agent and called it a universal agent for work. One prompt box carries the organization's business context. It plans the work, uses skills and tools, and connects to Cloud customers' business systems. It brings finished work back inside documents, the inbox, and developer environments. It picks the best model for the job, with built-in cost controls and enterprise security, admin, and governance.

Anthropic

Oct 7, 2026

Claude Haiku 5.5 is for high-volume work

On 7 October 2026 Anthropic introduced Claude Haiku 5.5 and called it the cheapest, fastest, and most capable small model it has released. It is for high-volume, cost-sensitive work such as summaries, compactions, database queries, and classification. It pairs with Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 as a subagent on coding work. It is the first Haiku-class model with an adjustable effort setting. It is available now on all platforms, including Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, and Microsoft Azure.

Claude Sonnet 5.5 is for scoped work

Anthropic released Sonnet 5.5 on 28 September 2026 as a faster, lower-cost complement to Opus 5.5 for well-scoped tasks such as bugs, documents, slides, and spreadsheets. In Claude Code and Anthropic's apps, the default effort is Medium.

Anthropic

Claude Code aliases now point at 5.5

On the Anthropic API, opus resolves to Opus 5.5 and sonnet resolves to Sonnet 5.5. Claude Platform on AWS still resolves sonnet to Sonnet 4.6. Bedrock and Google Cloud Agent Platform resolve it to Sonnet 4.5. Microsoft Foundry resolves opus to Opus 4.6 and sonnet to Sonnet 4.5. Sonnet 5.5 needs Claude Code v2.1.284 or later, and Opus 5.5 needs v2.1.280 or later.

ChatGPT dots keep working between chats

OpenAI describes dots as always-on agents in ChatGPT that take ongoing work and keep going between conversations. You set a goal, connect the apps, and define what the dot may do on its own. Rollout is gradual for eligible Pro and Business Premium users aged 18 or older. Pro excludes the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK at launch, and Enterprise is a beta that is off by default.
